Privacy

This is a satirical website. Here's what happens with your data.

Your location

If you allow location access, your coordinates are used in your browser to calculate distance and wind direction. They are not stored or sent to us. To look up wind, the site asks Open-Meteo for weather at the President's location, not yours.

Advertising and cookies

This site shows ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ad Settings, and learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and performance.
